## 📌 Executive Summary & Business Problem

Understanding customer purchasing behavior is essential for improving customer retention, increasing repeat purchases, and optimizing promotional strategies.

This project analyzes customer transaction and behavioral data to identify high-value customer segments, evaluate subscription behavior, understand discount sensitivity, and uncover purchasing patterns that can support targeted marketing and retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Data Pipeline & Technical Approach

* **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Standardized categorical variables, handled inconsistent customer attributes, transformed purchasing-frequency fields, and prepared transaction data for analysis using Pandas.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyzed purchasing behavior across age groups, subscription status, payment methods, product categories, and promotional-code usage.
* **Behavioral Analysis:** Compared purchase frequency, spending patterns, review ratings, subscription behavior, and discount usage across customer segments.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Used Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn to identify customer trends, behavioral relationships, and category-level purchasing patterns.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

* **Subscription Behavior:** Subscribers demonstrated higher purchasing engagement and spending compared with non-subscribed customers, indicating an opportunity to use membership programs to improve customer retention.
* **Discount Sensitivity:** Non-subscribed customers showed greater reliance on promotional discounts, highlighting the importance of understanding price sensitivity when designing retention campaigns.
* **Category Repeat Behavior:** Clothing and Footwear demonstrated stronger repeat-purchase patterns, while Accessories showed comparatively lower repeat activity after the initial purchase.
* **Customer Engagement:** Purchase frequency, subscription status, review behavior, and promotional usage provided useful signals for differentiating customer segments.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

* **Subscription Conversion:** Offer targeted membership trials or introductory benefits to frequent non-subscribed customers to encourage recurring purchases.
* **Lifecycle Re-Engagement:** Use post-purchase campaigns and personalized product recommendations to encourage customers to make repeat purchases.
* **Loyalty Program Optimization:** Use points, member benefits, and personalized rewards alongside discounts to improve retention while protecting product margins.
* **Segment-Based Marketing:** Develop targeted campaigns based on purchase frequency, product preferences, subscription status, and promotional sensitivity rather than applying the same strategy to every customer segment.
